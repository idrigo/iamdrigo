About

Oceanographer by training, meteorologist by trade

I studied oceanography at Moscow State University and went to sea as a hydrometeorology engineer. A Master's in applied maths and ML at ITMO got me forecasting Arctic sea ice with neural networks.

Since 2019 I've worked in commercial weather: Windy.app, four years at Yandex Weather, a stretch on my own, and now OpenWeather. Most of that time I was the meteorologist in the room when a client asked whether a forecast was good enough for their decision.

The science never stopped. Last winter I spent two and a half months in the tropical Atlantic measuring deep currents in the Vema Channel. My publications are on Google Scholar.
